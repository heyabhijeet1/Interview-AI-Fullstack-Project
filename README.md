# Interview AI

Interview AI is an interview preparation platform that helps job seekers turn a target job description into a personalized, practical study plan. It uses the role requirements and the candidate's experience to organize preparation around what is most relevant for the opportunity.

## Purpose

Preparing for an interview can feel broad and overwhelming. Candidates often have to figure out which skills matter, what questions they may face, and how to use their preparation time. Interview AI brings those steps together in one place, giving candidates a focused starting point tailored to the role they want.

## For job seekers

Share a job description and describe your experience, skills, and goals. You can also upload a PDF resume for additional context; a resume is optional. Interview AI creates a report with:

- A role match score and areas to strengthen
- Technical and behavioral interview questions, including what each question explores and suggested answer guidance
- Skill gaps to consider before the interview
- A day-by-day preparation roadmap
- An option to download a tailored resume as a PDF

You can try the product as a guest without signing up. Create an account to access reports from that guest session and keep your preparation reports together.

## Project highlights

- Responsive interface with light and dark themes
- Guest and registered account flows
- Persistent interview reports associated with user accounts
- Report generation using Google GenAI
- PDF creation with Puppeteer
- Protected report access through an Express API

## Technology

**Frontend:** React, Vite, Tailwind CSS  
**Backend:** Node.js, Express  
**Data:** MongoDB and Mongoose  
**AI and documents:** Google GenAI and Puppeteer
